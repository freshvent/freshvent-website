Service Area

Free in Richmond Hill & Markham

Our home zone covers Richmond Hill and Markham — service calls there are always free. Outside the zone, one simple distance fee applies.

Check your postal code

Enter your postal code to see instantly whether your address is in the free zone.

How the distance fee works

Outside Richmond Hill & Markham, a distance fee of $17.00 per hour of travel is added to your cart automatically when you enter your postal code — the first hour is applied at checkout, and you'll always see it as its own line item before you pay. No surprises, no "we'll see when we get there."

Try it: L4C (Richmond Hill) is free. M5V (downtown Toronto) adds the fee.

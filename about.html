About FreshVent

An HVAC company run like a platform — because it is one

FreshVent was built to fix the two things homeowners hate about HVAC: unanswered phones and mystery pricing. Our website, phone system, customer records and pricing all run as one integrated platform.

No missed calls

Our 24/7 voice-AI receptionist answers every call on the first ring, captures your equipment details, and hot-transfers true emergencies straight to the on-shift technician. In a heat wave or a cold snap, nobody gets voicemail.

No paperwork

From the first call to the final invoice, everything is logged automatically in our CRM — call transcripts, equipment history, quotes and receipts. You get a written summary after every job, not a carbon-copy slip.

Transparent pricing

Parts and small services are priced right here on the site. Big projects get a flat written quote before work starts. The only extra you'll ever see is the published $17/hr distance fee outside our free zone — shown before you pay, never after.

How the flywheel turns

Every part of the platform makes the next part better:

1 · Leads

Calls, forms and shop orders flow into one place — nothing is lost, ever.

2 · Automation

Triage, booking, reminders and records happen automatically, so humans can focus on the work.

3 · Install

Technicians arrive with your equipment history in hand and a flat quote already agreed.

4 · Profit

Fair margins, repeat customers and referrals fund better tools — and better prices — next season.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

The things homeowners ask us most — including exactly how the distance fee works.

If your home is outside our free zone (Richmond Hill & Markham), a distance fee of $17.00 per hour of travel applies. Enter your postal code in the cart or on the Service Area page and the fee is calculated and shown as its own line item before you pay — the first hour is applied at checkout. Inside the zone, service calls are always free.
